The game is not made, sponsored or supported by Telegram. By playing, you agree to these terms and to Telegram's own terms.

2. What you're buying

The shop sells digital items for use in the game: weapon and chassis crates, gem caches, the starter pack, paints, the Lunar Pass premium track, the Moonbeam weapon, the Lunar skin and Hyperspace Vault Gold. When you buy one, you get a personal license to use it in the game while the game runs. You don't own it as property.

  • Items and points have no cash value. They can't be sold, traded, transferred to another account or exchanged for money or crypto.
  • Prices are shown in Telegram Stars before you pay. TON and SOL checkout is paused; when it returns, prices are shown in that currency before you pay and SOL prices follow the TON price at live exchange rates.
  • The Lunar Pass premium track lasts one season: 30 days (Season 1, the launch season, runs until December 1, 2026). Rewards you don't claim before the season ends don't carry over.
  • Genesis Gold is a record of Gold deposited before $LUNAR launches. Any Genesis rewards are limited edition and have no cash value; their form, timing and whether they happen may change.
  • Hyperspace Vault Gold is not a currency. It can't be spent, sent or withdrawn; your lifetime Gold only sets your Vault level, which unlocks cosmetic items. It gives no stats, power or points. A refunded deposit removes its Gold, and any look that depended on it.
  • 25% of what the shop takes in goes into the planned $LUNAR launch liquidity pool. The rest funds running and developing the game, including servers, promotion and partnerships.

3. Not an investment

Buying anything in the game does not buy $LUNAR or any other token, and nothing in the game is an offer of a token or a promise of any financial return. The planned $LUNAR airdrop is based on leaderboard rank and days played, and verified spending may also be considered, with no guaranteed amount; its rules, timing and size may change or it may not happen. Don't spend money in the game expecting to make money.

4. Crates and odds

Some items are random. The crate screen shows the odds before you buy: for weapon and chassis crates, every item of that rarity you don't own yet is equally likely, and every stat roll from 105% to 110% is equally likely. For gem caches, each of the 6 core types is 1 in 6.

5. Refunds and payment support

  • For any problem with a purchase, send /paysupport to the bot, or message @LunarBattle with your Player ID (in the game: Settings, top right) and roughly when you paid. We reply within 48 hours.
  • Telegram's support team can't help with purchases made in this bot.
  • If you paid and the item didn't arrive, we deliver it or refund you. Telegram Stars refunds go back to your Stars balance through Telegram.
  • Crypto payments (TON, SOL) are final once confirmed on-chain, except where we refund a failed delivery. Check the amount and address your wallet shows before you approve.
  • Items that were delivered and used, or opened from a crate, generally can't be refunded, except where the law where you live says otherwise.

6. Fair play

One account per person. No cheats, bots, scripts, modified clients, exploiting bugs, or fake accounts to farm referral rewards. We can remove points, items, rank or airdrop eligibility from accounts that break these rules, and we can suspend them.

7. Changes to the game

We keep improving the game, so modes, maps, balance, items, rewards and prices can change. Live matches against other players follow these same rules. Our server decides their results and points. Leaving a live match, or staying disconnected when it ends, counts as a loss. We'll post major changes in the game or the Telegram channel.

8. Your data

We store what the game needs to run: your Telegram user ID, the name shown on the leaderboard, your game progress, your squad (invites), your purchase records (including payment IDs from Telegram or the blockchain), and a scrambled (hashed) code of your network address, used only to spot fake and duplicate accounts. We don't sell your data. If you allow it, the bot sends you one reminder a day; turn it off in Settings or send /stop. To ask for your data to be deleted, message @LunarBattle.

9. No guarantees

The game is provided as it is. It may have bugs or downtime, and it may be changed or shut down. To the extent the law allows, our total responsibility to you is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before a claim.

10. Age

If you're under 18, or under the age of majority where you live, get a parent's or guardian's permission before you buy anything.

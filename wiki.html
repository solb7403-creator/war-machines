The mission

LUNAR is the meme. War Machines is how it gets to orbit.

Most tokens launch the same tired way. A few insiders get in cheap, the chart rips, and everyone who shows up after is their exit.

We're flipping that. $LUNAR is a community token, and the community is whoever climbs into a tank and fights for it. You play, you stack points, you climb the board. When launch day comes, the people who put in the hours are the ones holding $LUNAR.

No VC round. No private presale. The founder's tokens sit in one public, labeled wallet and unlock slowly over a year, same rules for everyone to see.

How you get a seat on the rocket

  • Finish in the top 1,000 on the overall leaderboard at the snapshot. You earn your allocation.
  • Show up. Allocation size counts your points and the days you played. One crazy weekend won't beat a pilot who logs in every day.
  • You can buy better gear. You can't buy rank. The board only counts what you earn in the arena.

How to play

Drag. Dodge. Delete.

  1. Open the game in Telegram. Your save lives on your Telegram account, so switching phones costs you nothing.
  2. On the Play tab, tap Change to pick a game type, team size (1v1, 2v2, 3v3) and map. Or let Random pick for you.
  3. Hit FIGHT. The start screen shows who you're up against and lets you swap chassis to counter them.
  4. Drag anywhere to drive. Your cannon aims and fires by itself. Your whole job is where you stand.
  5. Wreck the other team. Cash your points in at the Garage, come back meaner, queue again.

Combat

Your cannon aims itself. Staying alive is on you.

Bullets actually fly. They take time to land, so a tank that keeps moving eats a lot fewer of them. A dead-center hit can crit for double damage. A shot that just clips you is a graze and hurts less.

Supers. The thin bar under your health fills when you deal damage (fastest), take damage, or land crits. When it's full, tap the red Super button to auto-aim, or drag from it to aim it yourself. Every weapon line has its own super, and each one has a tell, so watch for the red warning lane. Enemy supers beep while they charge.

Dash. The blue button bursts you forward in the direction you're driving. Time it right and you slip out of a super. It recharges in 4 seconds. The bots dash too.

Mystery boxes. Five drop per match at random spots, the first one 5 to 8 seconds in. Roll over it for a random buff. The buff sticks until you soak a set amount of damage (watch the ring around your tank drain). Same buffs stack, so greed is sometimes correct.

  • Meteor shower: red circles mark where rocks are about to hit. Get out, or eat 260 damage. They don't care whose team you're on.
  • Solar flare: for 20 seconds every super charges twice as fast.

Maps

Cover is real. Use it or lose to it.

Containers, domes, boulders and canyon walls block both bullets and tanks. A few things play by their own rules:

  • Supply crates (orange) break after enough hits, and sometimes drop a mystery box when they go.
  • Craters slow you to 60% speed. Cutting through one is a gamble.

Game types

Five ways to fight. One of them pays extra today.

Arena: one life each. Wipe the other team, or have more health left when the 3-minute clock runs out.

Arena 1v1 is a best of 3. First to 2 rounds wins, and the match pays double. Between rounds you see the chassis they're bringing next, and you get the last word: swap yours to counter it. Rounds run 90 seconds.

Moon Zone: park in the glowing circle to capture it. While your team owns it, you score. First to 100 wins, or whoever's ahead at the buzzer. One enemy tank sitting in your zone freezes your score, so you can't just camp and pray.

  • The zone moves. Every 20 to 28 seconds it jumps to a random spot. 6 seconds before, a gold ring shows where it is going. Rotate early to get there first.
  • Kills are +5 points for your team.
  • Respawn grows: 4s the first time, +1.5s for each extra death (max 9s). Throwing your tank away costs your team time on the zone.
  • Repair: off the zone, avoid damage for 3.5 seconds and your tank heals itself. No repairs while standing on the zone, so holding it wears you down.

Moon Zone matches run much longer than Arena, so they pay more per match: ×3 in 1v1, ×2.4 in 2v2, ×2.1 in 3v3.

Out of a team Arena match early? Tap Fast forward to watch the rest at triple speed.

Crystal RushNew: a crystal pops out of the mine in the middle every 6 seconds. Drive over it to carry it. When your team holds 10 or more, and more than the other team, a 15-second countdown starts. Survive it and you win. Get wrecked and every crystal you carry spills on the ground for whoever gets there first. 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3, 3 minutes, respawns and repairs like Moon Zone. Pays like Moon Zone: ×3, ×2.4, ×2.1.

BountyNew: every tank starts worth 2 stars. Wreck one and your team banks its stars, and your own bounty goes up by 1, to a max of 7. Get wrecked and yours drops back to 2. Most stars after 2 minutes wins. Tanks are lighter and respawn in 3 seconds, so kills keep coming. Hunt the 7-star tank; protect yours. Pays ×3.5 in 1v1, ×2.8 in 2v2, ×2.4 in 3v3.

Last StandingNew: you and 5 other pilots, every tank for itself, one life. From 15 seconds in, a ring closes toward a random spot near the middle; outside it you lose 4% of your health every second. Every wreck drops a mystery box, and 6 boxes are waiting at the start. Your match ends when you go down, so you can queue again right away. Pays by place, and 2nd and 3rd keep your win streak alive.

Random: can't decide? The game picks any of the five when the match starts.

Featured mode. Every day at 00:00 UTC one mode is featured: it pays +25% points and +10 Lunar Pass XP per match. It rotates Crystal Rush, Last Standing, Bounty, Crystal Rush, Bounty, Last Standing, Moon Zone. The Play tab and the setup screen show today's pick.

The featured mode also gets a live queue for the day: see Live matches below.

Live matchesNew

Real pilots, same rules. The server is the referee.

Arena (1v1 best of 3, 2v2, 3v3), Moon Zone and today's featured mode look for real pilots first. They carry a green Live tag on the setup screen. The other modes stay against AI pilots for now.

Quick Match is the fastest way to find real pilots: the green button under FIGHT. It joins everyone looking for a live match at your team size, whatever mode they picked. If someone chose Arena, you play Arena; if it's only Quick Match pilots, you get a random live mode. Maps are always random.

  • Matchmaking: same mode, same team size, similar gear (your league). If the room isn't full after 30 seconds, AI pilots take the empty seats. If nobody else turns up at all, you play AI pilots as usual. Play AI pilots now skips the wait.
  • You always know who's real. The VS screen says Live opponents or AI opponents, and every AI pilot carries an AI tag. In battle, live pilots wear their name.
  • The map is picked at random for live matches.
  • The server runs the fight and decides who won. It banks your points straight to the leaderboard, so nobody can fake a live result. Pay is the same as against AI: league, featured mode and win streak all count (live matches keep their own streak).
  • Lost connection? An AI pilot drives your tank until you're back, and the game reconnects by itself. Still gone 20 seconds after the match ends, or you leave, and it counts as a loss with no points. Leaving on the VS screen costs nothing.

Your very first match is always the coached one against AI pilots. Live matches need the game opened inside Telegram.

Garage

Where scrap metal turns into a war machine.

Upgrades cost 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 12,500 points for levels 1 to 4 (20,000 to max one, 100,000 to max all five). The first level is cheap, so start there.

  • Hull: +8% max HP per level
  • Cannon: +8% damage per level
  • Engine: +6% speed per level
  • Targeting: +2% crit chance per level
  • Reactor: +10% super charge per level

9 weapons and 9 chassis to hunt down. Every line comes in Common, Rare and Epic, and each version only drops from its own rarity's crate. Rare hits +10% over Common. Epic jumps to +32%. When you pull a better version of a line, it bolts on automatically.

Rolls. Every crate item rolls 105% to 110% of its listed stats, any value equally likely, so your Titanfall isn't quite the same as the next guy's. Found all 3 of a rarity? That crate turns into a re-roll: it picks one of them and keeps whichever roll is better.

Tune-up. Own a crate item with a so-so roll? Re-roll it with points in the gear list: 3,000 for Common, 10,000 for Rare, 30,000 for Epic. It rolls 105 to 110% again and you keep the better one.

Salvage Yard (points only). Junkyard rebuilds of Rare and Epic gear for players saving up. Buy again to chase a better roll; you always keep your best. Every crate item beats every salvaged item:

Quests

Free points for stuff you were going to do anyway.

Daily questsNew. Four new ones every day at 00:00 UTC: two easy (100 to 120 points), one medium (160 to 180) and one hard (260 to 300), picked from 28 kinds. Wins, kills, damage, supers, boxes, Quick Matches, winning without being destroyed, 3 wins in a row, a 2-0 sweep, winning Last Standing and more. Finish all four for a +250 bonus. Don't like one? Swap it once a day for another of the same difficulty (twice with the premium pass). Each claim also gives Lunar Pass XP.

Weekly questsNew. Five a week (Monday to Sunday, UTC), the same five for every pilot, worth 750 to 3,750 points each and +150 pass XP. Two are there every week: play on 5 different days (1,500) and play 30 Quick Matches (3,750). The other three rotate from 14 kinds: win 20 matches, destroy 50 tanks, win in 4 different modes, win 5 in a row, win Last Standing on 3 different days, win 2 matches without being destroyed on 4 different days, capture the Moon Zone 30 times, play 16 featured-mode matches, fire 60 supers and more. Multi-day quests count a day once you hit that day's goal.

Weekly chest. Claim 20 daily quests in a week and the chest pays 2,000 points and a rare gem cache.

Your login streak bonus (up to +30%) applies to every daily quest, the all-four bonus, weekly quests and the chest. With a 7-day streak, a full day of quests pays about 1,200 points and a full week of weekly quests and the chest about 14,500.

Squad (referrals)

Bring your crew. Get paid every time they show up.

Grab your invite link in the Quests tab and send it anywhere. Anyone who opens the game with it for the first time joins your squad. Sign-ups alone pay nothing. You get paid when they actually stick around:

Days active means separate days played. Squad points count toward the 20,000 daily cap and the leaderboard. Alt accounts get pulled from the airdrop.

FAQ

Stuff people keep asking in the chat.

Who gets the airdrop?
Top 1,000 on the leaderboard at the snapshot, on a clean account. Play, climb the board, you’re in.

Does buying stuff get me more $LUNAR?
No. The shop sells gear. Tokens come from your rank and the days you played.

Do I need to spend money?
Nope. Free players can earn the full allocation — points come from playing. Spending is optional, but if you do spend, it's tracked and factored into your allocation too.

Am I playing real people?
In Arena, Moon Zone and the featured mode, yes, whenever other pilots are queuing. The VS screen tells you: Live opponents or AI opponents, with an AI tag on every AI pilot.

What's the featured mode?
One mode a day pays +25% points and extra Lunar Pass XP. It changes at 00:00 UTC.

Do paints change my stats?
No. They only change how your tank looks.

When's launch?
After the pre-launch seasons wrap. The snapshot date gets announced in the TG channel first.

Do I lose progress if I switch phones?
No. Your points, gear and quests live on the server, tied to your Telegram account.

Why didn't my rank update right away?
The server counts your points as soon as a match ends, and the leaderboard rebuilds every 5 minutes. Give it a moment.

My connection dropped at the end of a match. Did I lose the points?
No. The game keeps the result and sends it when you're back online, and it counts once. A match that starts with no connection can't be scored, though.

A result said “Not counted”. Why?
The server couldn't check it: the match ended faster than it can be played, its ticket expired (30 minutes), or there was no connection when it started. Over 40 matches in one hour also stop counting until the next hour.

Can I turn off the sound?
Yes, in Settings (the sliders icon, top right). Sound also follows your phone's silent switch.

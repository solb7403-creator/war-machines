The mission

LUNAR is the meme. War Machines is how it gets to orbit.

Most tokens launch the same tired way. A few insiders get in cheap, the chart rips, and everyone who shows up after is their exit.

We're flipping that. $LUNAR is a community token, and the community is whoever climbs into a tank and fights for it. You play, you stack points, you climb the board. When launch day comes, the people who put in the hours are the ones holding $LUNAR.

No VC round. No private presale. The founder's tokens sit in one public, labeled wallet and unlock slowly over a year, same rules for everyone to see.

  • Finish in the top 1,000 on the overall leaderboard at the snapshot. Your allocation is guaranteed.
  • Show up. Allocation size counts your points and the days you played. One crazy weekend won't beat a pilot who logs in every day.
  • Free works. Gear helps you win, but rank is earned in the arena. Nobody buys their way onto the board.

How to play

Drag. Dodge. Delete.

  1. Open the game in Telegram. Your save lives on your Telegram account, so switching phones costs you nothing.
  2. Head to the Arena, pick 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3, and pick a game type (or hit RANDOM and live a little).
  3. Drag anywhere to drive. Your cannon aims and fires by itself. Your whole job is where you stand.
  4. Wreck the other team, or have more health left when the 3-minute clock runs out.
  5. Cash your points in at the Garage, come back meaner, queue again.

Combat

Your cannon aims itself. Staying alive is on you.

Bullets actually fly. They take time to land, so a tank that keeps moving eats a lot fewer of them. A dead-center hit can crit for double damage. A shot that just clips you is a graze and hurts less.

Supers. The meter over your tank fills when you deal damage (fastest), take damage, or land crits. Full meter, smash the red button. Every weapon line has its own super, and each one has a tell, so watch for the red warning lane.

Matches run 3 minutes.

Mystery boxes. Five drop per match at random spots, the first one 15 to 30 seconds in. Roll over it for a random buff. The buff sticks until you soak a set amount of damage (watch the ring around your tank drain). Same buffs stack, so greed is sometimes correct.

Game types

Smash them, or out-hold them.

Arena: one life each. Wipe the other team, or have more health left when the clock runs out.

Moon Zone: park in the glowing circle to capture it. While your team owns it, you score. First to 100 wins, or whoever's ahead at the buzzer. One enemy tank sitting in your zone freezes your score, so you can't just camp and pray.

  • The zone moves. Every 20 to 28 seconds it jumps to a random spot. 6 seconds before, a gold ring shows where it is going. Rotate early to get there first.
  • Kills are +5 points for your team.
  • Respawn grows: 4s the first time, +1.5s for each extra death (max 9s). Throwing your tank away costs your team time on the zone.
  • Repair: off the zone, avoid damage for 3.5 seconds and your tank heals itself. No repairs while standing on the zone, so holding it wears you down. Losing a fight? Back off, heal, come back.

Points per match are the same as Arena.

Random: can't decide? The game picks Arena or Moon Zone when the match starts.

Overpass map: the bridge is solid. Drive up a ramp (gold arrows) to reach the deck and shoot down from high ground. Two tunnels run underneath: tanks in a tunnel can't be hit from the deck, and can't shoot up.

Garage

Where scrap metal turns into a war machine.

Upgrades cost 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 points for levels 1 to 4. Cheap early, so do them first.

  • Hull: +8% max HP per level
  • Cannon: +8% damage per level
  • Engine: +6% speed per level
  • Targeting: +2% crit chance per level
  • Reactor: +10% super charge per level

9 weapons and 9 chassis to hunt down. Every line comes in Common, Rare and Epic, and each version only drops from its own rarity's crate. Rare hits +10% over Common. Epic jumps to +32%. When you pull a better version of a line, it bolts on automatically.

Rolls. Every crate item rolls 100% to 110% of its listed stats, so your Titanfall isn't quite the same as the next guy's. Found all 3 of a rarity? That crate turns into a re-roll: it picks one of them and keeps whichever roll is better.

Salvage Yard (points only). Junkyard rebuilds of Rare and Epic gear, carrying 50% to 75% of the real item's boost. 5,000 points for Salvaged Rare, 20,000 for Salvaged Epic. Buy again to chase a better roll, you always keep your best. The real thing still beats it.

Cores are permanent boosts you socket into your machine. Always on, every match:

Cores stack with mystery-box buffs. Shards from Gem Caches level them up (Common 1 to 2 shards, Rare 3 to 4, Epic 6 to 9). Every level hits harder than the last: Lv2 ×1.2, Lv3 ×1.45, Lv4 ×1.8, Lv5 ×2.25.

Is the airdrop actually guaranteed?
Top 1,000 at the snapshot, on a clean account: yes.

No. The shop sells gear. Tokens come from your rank and the days you played.

Do I need to spend money?
Nope. Gear gives you an edge, but positioning wins fights and points come from playing.

When's launch?
After the pre-launch seasons wrap. The snapshot date gets announced in the TG channel first.

Do I lose progress if I switch phones?
No. Your save rides on your Telegram account.

Why didn't my rank update right away?
The leaderboard syncs about every 30 seconds and refreshes once a minute. Give it a sec.

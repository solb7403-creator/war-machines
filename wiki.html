The mission

LUNAR is the meme. War Machines is how it gets to orbit.

Most tokens launch the same way: insiders get in cheap, and everyone after them is their exit. $LUNAR flips that. The tokens go to whoever climbs into a tank and fights for them. No VC round, no private presale. The founder's tokens sit in one public wallet and unlock slowly over a year.

  • Finish in the top 1,000 on the leaderboard at the snapshot to earn an allocation.
  • Show up. Allocation size counts your points and the days you played.
  • You can buy gear, not points. Every point on the board is earned by playing: matches, quests and streaks.

How to play

Drag. Dodge. Delete.

  1. Open the game in Telegram. Your progress lives on your Telegram account.
  2. On Play, choose one of your two engineered machines. Tap Change to pick a mode, team size (1v1, 2v2, 3v3) and map, then hit FIGHT. Your selected complete build stays locked for the match.
  3. Drag anywhere to drive. Your cannon aims and fires by itself; your job is where you stand.
  4. Spend your points in the Garage and go again.

Combat

Your cannon aims itself. Staying alive is on you.

  • Keep moving. Bullets take time to land. A dead-center hit can crit for double damage.
  • Super: fills as you deal and take damage. Tap it to fire, or drag from it to aim. Watch for the red warning lane of enemy supers.
  • Shield: 3 seconds where you take only 35% damage. Recharges in 25 seconds.
  • Dash: a quick burst in the direction you're driving. Recharges in 4 seconds.
  • Lock a target: tap an enemy. Tap again, or tap the ground, to release.
  • Mystery boxes drop during every match: damage, armor, fire rate, speed, crit or repair, until you take enough damage.
  • Events: supply drops, meteor showers (get out of the red circles) and solar flares (supers charge twice as fast).
  • Cover is real. Walls and rocks block bullets. Orange crates break. Craters slow you down. Overpass has a bridge with tunnels underneath.

Game types

Six ways to fight. One of them pays extra today.

  • Arena: one life each. Wipe the other team. 1v1 is a best of 3.
  • Moon Zone: hold the glowing circle to score. First to 100 wins. The zone moves.
  • Crystal Rush: grab crystals from the mine. Hold 10+ (and more than them) for 15 seconds to win.
  • Bounty: kills earn stars. The more kills in a row, the more you're worth. Most stars wins.
  • Last Standing: 6 tanks, every tank for itself, a closing ring. Pays the most.
  • Survival: you (and AI wingmen) against a giant megatank. Last the clock or destroy it.

Featured mode: one mode a day pays +25% points. Live matches: Arena, Moon Zone and the featured mode look for real players first (Quick Match is the fastest way in). The VS screen always says whether you're facing live players or AI.

Garage

Where scrap metal turns into a war machine.

Build: 5 stats, up to 14 levels in total. Hull (+8% HP), Cannon (+8% damage), Engine (+6% speed), Targeting (+2% crit) and Reactor (+10% super charge) per level. Reset any time: half your points back, or pay a small fee in Stars, TON or SOL to get all of them back.

Weapons and chassis: 3 lines each, in Common, Rare (+14%) and Epic (+34%). Every crate item rolls 105–110% of its stats. Re-roll one you own with Tune-up, or buy cheaper Salvaged gear with points.

Be on the list when the countdown hits zero.

FAQ

Do I need to spend money?
No. Free players can earn the full allocation.

Am I playing real people?
In live modes, when others are queuing. The VS screen tells you, and AI pilots are tagged.

What's a ghost?
When you're offline, your tank can fill an AI seat in someone else's match. It earns you nothing and costs you nothing; you'll see what it did when you're back.

When's launch?
After the pre-launch seasons. The snapshot date is announced in the news channel first.

What if I lose connection in a live match?
An AI pilot drives your tank while the game reconnects. If you're still gone 20 seconds after the match ends, or you leave, it counts as a loss with no points. Leaving on the VS screen costs nothing.

Why didn't my rank update?
The leaderboard rebuilds every 5 minutes.

A result said "Not counted". Why?
The match couldn't be verified: it ended too fast, the ticket expired, or there was no connection when it started. Over 40 matches an hour also stop counting until the next hour.

Do I lose progress if I switch phones?
No. It's tied to your Telegram account.

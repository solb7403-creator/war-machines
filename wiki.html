How to play

  1. Open the game in Telegram. Your progress is tied to your Telegram account, so it follows you across devices.
  2. Pick a mode in the Arena: 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3.
  3. Drag to drive. Your cannon aims and fires on its own; your job is positioning, dodging and grabbing boxes.
  4. Win by destroying the enemy, or by having more health left when the 3-minute timer runs out.
  5. Spend points in the Garage to upgrade, then queue again.

Combat

Shots are physical projectiles. They travel, so moving makes you harder to hit. Direct hits can crit for double damage; shots that only clip the edge are grazes and deal less.

Super attack. The meter above your tank fills as you deal damage (fastest), take damage, and land crits. When it's full, hit the red button. Each weapon has its own super.

Matches last 3 minutes.

Mystery boxes drop at random spots, 5 per match, starting 15–30 seconds in. Drive over one to claim a random buff. A buff lasts until you've absorbed a set amount of damage (the ring around your tank drains as it wears off). Buffs of the same type stack.

Garage

Upgrades cost 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 points for levels 1–4.

  • Hull: +8% max HP per level
  • Cannon: +8% damage per level
  • Engine: +6% speed per level
  • Targeting: +2% crit chance per level
  • Reactor: +10% super charge per level

9 weapons and 9 chassis to collect. Each line has a Common, Rare and Epic version, and each version drops only from that rarity's cache. Same line, higher rarity is always stronger (Rare +12%, Epic +28%). Your best version of a line is equipped automatically when you find it.

FAQ

Will I lose progress if I switch phones?
No. Progress is saved to your Telegram account.

Why didn't my rank update right away?
The leaderboard syncs in steps every ~30 seconds and refreshes once a minute.

Do I need to spend money?
No. Gear gives an edge, but skill and positioning win fights.

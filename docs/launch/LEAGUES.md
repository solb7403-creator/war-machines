# Leagues and AI difficulty (Session 2, Nov 2, 2026)

All numbers live in `js/rules.js` as one-line constants so beta data can retune them. Measurements: real phone code
(`dev/tests/combat-sim.mjs` world), all 6 maps, 4 fixed seeds per map (24 matches per cell; 264 per kit average over the
11 mode x team-size setups). Bot pilots are a proxy for live play. "Today" = `origin/main` 8df6500 with the same harness.

## The rule

- **7 leagues**: Rookie, Pilot, Veteran, Ace, Elite, Master, Lunar Legend (no clash with chassis, weapon, mode, tier or tag names).
- **Form** `S.lwr`: moving average of results, starts 0.5; win 1, draw or Last Standing top 3 0.5, loss or quit 0;
  `lwr += (r - lwr) / 20` (`LG_FORM_N`). Updated by `ecoAfterMatch` / `ecoQuit` (rules.js) on every path: AI tickets
  (`eco.js matchEnd`), live rooms (`eco.js creditMatch`), the phone's local economy. Server authoritative (`ECO_KEYS` lwr, lgi, lg, lgb).
- **League rating** = `power x (0.8 + 0.4 x form)` (`LG_FORM_W` 0.4); under 10 matches the form counts as 0.5.
- **Lines** (geometric, equal % steps): Pilot from `LG_FIRST` 84, each next line x`LG_RATIO` 1.21:
  Pilot 84, Veteran 101.6, Ace 123, Elite 148.8, Master 180.1, Lunar Legend 217.9. Promote at the line, drop `LG_DEMOTE` 4% below it (`S.lgi`).
  Power range after items 1-3: stock 68, common 80, rare 106, Epic 164, max free-to-play 213, full Legendary ladder kit 235.
- **AI step**: `AI power = min(your power, aiPower(power) x aiStep(league, team size))`, `aiStep = 1 + (LG_AI[league] - 1) x LG_TEAM_W`,
  `LG_AI = 1.04^league` (1.00 Rookie ... 1.265 Lunar Legend). Team weights `LG_TEAM_W`: 1v1 1.0, 2v2 0.6, Last Standing 0.3, **3v3 0** (with 0.1 a single AI tier step, e.g. Epic's AI 125 -> 126, moved
  3v3 win rates 7-12 points at 72 matches and failed the +/-4 check; at 0, 3v3 plays exactly today's AI in every league).
  The base curve `aiPower` is today's (68 x (power/68)^AI_EXP; AI_EXP refit to 0.70 for the new power scale so each ladder kit meets
  the same AI tier as before, +/-1); the 10% base allowance was **not** used. Survival keeps `AI_BOSS_EXP` (already at your power).
  AI pilots fly authored loadouts (`AI_TIERS`, 33 tiers, 68 -> 181; the top two are full 14-level Epic builds with a mod; never
  Moonbeam or Lunar Skin; never above your power). Inside a league the AI never changes with your results.
- **Pay per league**: base pay `LG_BASE_PAY` x1.00/1.09/1.18/1.28/1.39/1.50/1.60 (base points: airdrop, boards, daily cap) and
  the spendable multiplier `LG_MULT` x1.00/1.15/1.30/1.45/1.60/1.80/2.00 on top (spendable only). Cap: 20,000 base points a day in every league.
- New-player protections unchanged (first 3 matches gentler, first 10 no counter-chassis). Ghost rules unchanged.
  The VS screen says which league the AI is set for.

## On screen (display only)
- **Top bar, every tab**: a chip with the league's shield and name (`#lgChip`, `renderTop`); it shortens "Lunar Legend" to "Legend" only when the bar can't fit it (then the tier chip shows its diamond alone first). Tap or Enter opens the ladder.
- **Ranks**: "Your league" card under the tier box: league rating, a bar from the drop line to the next line (a tick marks this league's line), distance to the next league and above the drop line, form as a percentage. "All leagues" opens the ladder: 7 rungs, Lunar Legend on top, You marked, each with its line, base pay, spendable bonus and AI step (`openLeagues`).
- **Leaderboard**: every All time / Today / This week row and your pinned row shows a league pill.

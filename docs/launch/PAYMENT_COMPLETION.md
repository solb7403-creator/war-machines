## Integrated contract and source changes

- `worker/src/ton-orders.js` retains and extends the existing CAS issuer for both currencies. Server-controlled canonical buyer, goods/TON+Stars price, network, recipient, integer base units (`amountNano`: nanoTON or lamports), 128-bit nonce, unique memo, expiry and day/week/season persist atomically. SOL conversion preserves the original 3/4-decimal source rounding; conversion outages refuse new quotes. Retried request IDs cannot switch goods/currency/network/recipient or extend expiry, and recover the old quote even during a later price-feed outage.
- `worker/src/chain-payments.js` implements issuance, status, one-attempt pre-wallet intent, cancellation, settlement, permanent network/transaction ownership claims and audited refund observation. Inventory, entitlement eligibility and receipt/journal mutations share the economy CAS. Quotes and actual paid receipts survive app closure. Wallet errors never prove “not sent”; one order permits one persisted approval attempt. A cancelled attempt retains its reservation until expiry. Late-paid, amount-mismatched or newly ineligible receipts remain reviewable with no goods. A delayed confirmation of an on-time eligible transfer may settle normally.
- Stars precheckout and both chain issuers share the same account reservation checks. Competing rails have one CAS winner. Grant-time checks still catch ownership/deal/season changes and delayed overlapping payments. There is no browser-priced grant or unchecked local-web inventory path.
- `worker/src/chain-providers.js` is the only chain evidence boundary. It calls fixed HTTPS endpoints with eight-second timeouts; client bodies cannot inject an evidence object or arbitrary provider URL. `settleChainOrder` accepts an injectable lookup only as a library/testing argument, never an HTTP body argument.
- `worker/src/payment-ledger.js` extracts the existing idempotent Stars/revenue logic and keeps versioned two-receipt reconciliation. Chain refund projections are terminal; sale and reversal each have permanent revenue event IDs. Orphan pending markers are retained and reported, while valid work behind them can proceed. Prices, goods, reward math and 25% public LP share are unchanged. Chain revenue is frozen from the server's live TON/USD quote, without stale-price fallback; mismatched cash receipts are accounted proportionally and held, not granted. This moves chain valuation sampling from confirmation time to immutable quote time.
- `worker/src/payment-jobs.js` adds a per-account `PaymentJob` Durable Object. A durable wake/alarm precedes issuance or payment mutation. Alarms do one bounded unit: receipt accounting, verified-wallet identity projection, or one order lookup. They re-arm before work, survive outages/restarts, preserve existing shared-payer clustering semantics, and back off old unconfirmed orders. No global account scan or buyer return is required. A crash between wake and account commit still has a low-frequency wake, rather than losing the intent.
- `worker/src/payment-ops.js`, `/web/payment-support.html` and its script provide passkey-authenticated, canonical-PID allowlisted dry-run/explicit-confirmation/audit. Operators may annotate holds, retry a bounded accounting batch, or requeue a known authoritative historical receipt without granting goods or inventing aggregate adjustments. Crypto refund recording requires an already finalized exact outgoing transfer from the configured recipient to the original payer, a unique refund memo/reference and replay protection. **No endpoint signs, broadcasts, issues a Telegram refund or sends value.** Refunds still require separate owner authorization outside this tool.
- `worker/src/index.js` connects the routes, refuses every legacy orderless `/api/pay-log` crypto request, preserves original Stars quote ownership even for mismatched callbacks, holds expired/cancelled paid quotes, and adds Stars cancellation/status and dedicated test-API support. `worker/src/web-auth.js` tags genuinely passkey-issued sessions with `authMethod:'passkey'`.
- `/web/checkout.html`, `.js`, `.css` implement exact quotes, server history, wallet approval intent, pending/cancelled/review/paid/refunded screens and close/reopen recovery. TON Connect receives server destination/amount/comment/network/expiry. SOL uses the real pinned Web3 SDK to build native transfer + unique readonly reference + memo and an injected Phantom/Solflare/Backpack-compatible provider. Safari without an injected provider offers the independent page in Phantom's wallet browser; that browser must authenticate the same account. No fictitious direct Safari wallet-signing API is assumed. SDK versions and SHA-384 integrity are pinned.
- `js/core.js`, `js/net.js`, `js/ui.js`, `web/game.js` enforce Stars-only Telegram purchase UI, remove the crypto alternative suggestion, route independent web purchases to checkout, and retain server-authoritative grants. The old standalone `pay.html` stays paused; its unbound links are not revived. New direct checkout is `/web/checkout.html`, not that legacy page.
- `worker/package.json` pins `@ton/core@0.62.0` for address/BOC parsing. `worker/wrangler.toml` adds source-only Node compatibility and the `PAYMENTS`/`v3-payment-recovery` SQLite binding/migration. Nothing was deployed or migrated.

### Authentication and policy boundary

Crypto and operator APIs require a first-party HttpOnly website session issued by the passkey flow, canonical account identity, exact origin and CSRF. Telegram `initData`, ambiguous identities, Telegram website/OIDC sessions and client `isWebsite` flags cannot authorize them. New routes are default-off. Telegram selectors offer only XTR; there is no Telegram-to-crypto sales redirect.

HTTP cannot cryptographically attest whether a native app hosts a browser. Final independent-site/shared-entitlement policy, approved host/RP and native-webview behavior therefore still need platform/policy and physical-device sign-off; do not claim these checks make an in-Telegram crypto sale permitted. [Telegram's policy](https://core.telegram.org/bots/payments-stars) requires Stars for digital goods inside bots/Mini Apps. Activation remains blocked pending that sign-off.

## Proof evidence and assumptions

**TON:** actual selected schema inspected from `toncenter/ton-indexer` commit `a677b0905c9923cf48380eee7b76b1fe3e4d2b8f`, `ton-index-go/index/models/blockchain.go` and `response.go`, and compared with public testnet responses. The adapter requires matching account/destination and exact nonzero integer value, opcode-zero BOC comment, explicit non-emulated normal successful execution, non-bounced input, transaction hash/time, indexed `mc_block_seqno`, a matching masterchain block with expected `global_id`, and a later masterchain head. Missing/unknown evidence never grants. The supported receiving contract is an initialized standard wallet with those positive execution phases; other phases remain pending for review rather than guessed successful. TON's [finality guidance](https://docs.ton.org/applications/payments/overview) identifies masterchain inclusion as finality.

`dev/tests/wtest/fixtures/ton-testnet-observed.json` records a **pre-existing unrelated public testnet** transaction, indexed in masterchain block 94805681, observed with later head 94806108 and `global_id=-3`. It is rejected for a fresh order. Synthetic positive order fixtures are separately labeled. No TON wallet was funded or used by this session.

**SOL:** `getTransaction` requests `jsonParsed` and **finalized**; signature status must independently say finalized, execution errors must be null and slots must agree. Full genesis hashes, transaction signature, time, native System transfer/program, signer, exact destination/lamports, unique reference and Memo instruction must match the order. Historical balance deltas alone authorize nothing. The original zero-value/unrelated-history exploit remains rejected. See [RPC docs](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettransaction).

Public read-only `getGenesisHash` calls returned full devnet `EtWTRABZaYq6iMfeYKouRu166VU2xqa1wcaWoxPkrZBG` and mainnet `5eykt4UsFv8P8NJdTREpY1vzqKqZKvdpKuc147dw2N9d`; shortened chain IDs were caught in RED tests and corrected. No test funds arrived, keys were not retained, and **zero transfers were broadcast**. That alternative faucet's account requirements/availability were not verified. These observations establish connectivity/schema only, not a purchase.

Both adapters trust the selected HTTPS provider's chain attestations; they are not independent consensus/light-client proofs. Compromised providers, production quotas/indexing latency, archived history and real merchant configuration need external validation. ## Flags, support workflow and remaining gates

**No production enablement:** absent `CRYPTO_CHECKOUT_ENABLED` fails closed. Isolated testing requires `CRYPTO_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=test`, `PAYMENT_NETWORK=testnet`, separately controlled `TON_TEST_RECIPIENT` / `SOL_TEST_RECIPIENT`, and the `PAYMENTS` binding. This maps to TON testnet and Solana devnet. Mainnet requires the separately reserved `approved-mainnet` flag plus `PAYMENT_NETWORK=mainnet`; neither was set. Configured mainnet owner recipients are preserved. `STARS_TEST_MODE=1` requires isolated `PAYMENT_NETWORK=testnet` and uses `/bot<TOKEN>/test/...`; it needs a dedicated test-environment bot token, not the production token.

Operator access additionally requires `PAYMENT_OPS_ENABLED=1` and `PAYMENT_OPERATOR_PIDS` containing explicitly authorized canonical passkey account IDs. `/web/payment-support.html`: select one buyer/action/receipt, enter reason, dry-run, inspect plan, then confirm its exact revision fingerprint. No implicit batch approval, username role, mass scan, automatic refund or historical inventory grant exists. `requeue-receipt` requires a permanent authoritative account receipt. Unpriced/uncertain pre-journal history, unknown aggregate discrepancies and absent authoritative receipts require evidence review; the tool deliberately refuses to guess or fabricate a repair. Historical legacy TON pending payments require an audited support/cutover policy before deployment because their old references cannot securely be upgraded into bound orders.

Limits preserved explicitly: 32 unresolved orders per account fail closed (settled purchases do not exhaust it); 100 operator audits per account fail closed. Evidence is never silently deleted to clear capacity. Permanent receipts/claims, orphan markers and pre-journal failures need retention/archival/monitoring and approved operations. Alarms back off old unconfirmed payments; provider pagination can delay discovery through a large backlog. Production provider capacity, indexing delays, and full worst-case Free-plan traffic remain uncertified. These are operations/capacity sign-off items, not permission to enable an unsafe fallback.

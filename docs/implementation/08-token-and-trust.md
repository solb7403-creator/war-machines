# 08 — Token-launch integrity and community trust

Priority: P1, launch gate. Formalize airdrop eligibility, snapshot challenge handling, referral allocation, on-chain liquidity accounting and token supply distribution; existing docs describe a 1 billion supply and specific pool shares but actual launch execution remains a separate process.

Implementation: publish canonical, versioned eligibility and cutoff rules with snapshot time, active-day weighting, ranking tie breaks, fair-play flag review and appeals. Distinguish player top-1000 allocation from any separate referral allocation; prevent double counting and linked-account farming. Produce reproducible snapshot exports and independent audit hashes, wallet provenance and verified payment-to-LP ledger. Publish vesting, allocations, claim rules, risk disclosures and regional eligibility only after independent legal and security review. Do not conflate stars/points with guaranteed cash rewards or imply token-price appreciation.

Acceptance: snapshot can be regenerated deterministically; flagged and cleared accounts receive documented treatment; linked-wallet clusters reviewed; ledger reconciles paid/refunded/fees/LP transfers; multisig/treasury controls tested; audited claims resist replay/sybil attacks; community-facing disclosures match deployed code.

## Operations plan — Release 08
**Depends on:** 03 account identity, 04 reliable population/engagement, 05 game fairness, 07 verified revenue ledger. Legal/security diligence starts **now**, not at the end.

- [ ] **08.1 Freeze canonical facts:** reconcile wiki, game rules, existing treasury and airdrop documents: token chain/supply (currently described as Solana / 1 billion), allocation percentages, vesting, liquidity claims, current public wallet, seasons and founder disclosures. Flag contradictions instead of quietly changing them.
- [ ] **08.2 Distribution model:** document exact top-1,000 cutoff, snapshot time and timezone, eligibility, weighting of lifetime points/active days/spend, anti-sybil flag appeal and tie-breaks. Specify the planned separate referral recognition for approximately 250 eligible participants with explicit allocation source and **no double-counted token supply**; do not invent missing percentages.
- [ ] **08.3 Deterministic snapshots:** produce read-only, reproducible ranking/credit outputs from authoritative player records, flag/appeal state and durable manifests with versioned hash. Test top-1000 tie boundaries, appeals, inactive/linked accounts, reruns and disaster recovery.
- [ ] **08.4 Sybil/false-positive fairness:** retain account linking and bot defenses, but audit carrier NAT/shared household/device false positives. Provide documented human review and appeal outcome; do not automatically withhold allocation permanently from innocent users.
- [ ] **08.5 Finance:** build verified settled-payment → net proceeds → 25% liquidity accounting, including transaction fees, refunds and transfers to announced treasury. Reconcile public on-chain receipts to backend records; do not display estimated LP as already deposited.
- [ ] **08.6 Token technical design:** choose token contract/mint, allocation/vesting contracts, liquidity custody, multisig/key management, signer recovery, on-chain publication and rollback/incident plan; commission independent audit before production launch.
- [ ] **08.7 Legal/platform gate:** engage qualified counsel for digital-asset issuance, airdrop eligibility, sweepstakes/gaming and consumer disclosures, tax/accounting, AML/sanctions considerations as applicable, and Apple/Google/Telegram restrictions. Publish transparent risk disclosures; no return or guaranteed-profit promises.
- [ ] **08.8 Launch checklist:** sandbox claim drill, penetration review, public allocation methodology, vesting/treasury addresses, support and disputes process, tokenomics FAQ matching deployed contracts, signed go/no-go approvals. Do not launch, distribute, transfer or lock assets without explicit owner authorization.

**Definition of done:** a reproducible, transparent, legally reviewed and externally verified token-launch package with no unaccounted supply, opaque money flow or unfair allocation.

== OWNER DECISIONS (Nov 21) ==
1. Theater: all 20 territories are in the war with the blueprint's numbers (start 16 human / 4 Parasite, Parasite floor 3, ceiling 8, Heartland never captured). Until 7-V builds their ground, the Parasites attack only the human-held territories among the 11 that exist today; the 7 new ones show on the War Map as safe human ground. The second bastion and the Citadel stay Parasite-held. Track each territory's living-terraform state for 7-V to draw. The six-sector Frontline keeps its own count beside the Moon's; no code that carries over or protects existing captures (overrides the blueprint's "every existing capture preserved").
2. War clock: a tick every 6 hours. Each assault and counterattack is announced 12 hours (two ticks) ahead and can be fought any time until it lands. Tick length and warning length are single settings.
3. Fighting in person: a win holds the territory, entrenches it for 24 hours and keeps its fortification. A loss counts as if you hadn't come: the normal strength roll decides and wear applies. One attempt per assault. The same applies when joining an LDF counterattack. (Overrides "your result decides it" for losses.)
4. Limits: at most one territory changes hands per tick; battles you didn't fight cost at most 2 territories net per day, whether you logged in or not; after one full day away, the front holds until you return (overrides "three days"); Parasites never above 8; accomplishments are permanent.
5. Strength: each point of DEFENSE over THREAT is worth the same: even 50%, +1 about 70%, +2 about 84%, +3 about 93%, the same in reverse when behind; always between 5% and 95%. Each Citadel tier cleared adds THREAT. LDF counterattacks use the same rule in reverse. Planning pick: shared-war morale counts 0 until a shared-war result exists. A simulation test, tuned through the garrison, general and LDF numbers: a front left alone drifts toward the Parasites about one territory every 2–3 days, never past the limits; a pilot who fights about half the assaults and fortifies the threatened territory each day pushes it back within a week.
6. Fortify: 5 levels per territory, +1 DEFENSE each. Level 1 costs about half a day of a regular player's Conquest income (economy-model regular profile); each level costs 2.4x the last, times the ring's danger factor (1.0 / 1.2 / 1.45), in alloy, He-3 and CP; one fortification queued at a time, on the town's build timers. Fortifying all 12 capturable human territories to level 5 takes a regular about a year before wear. Wear: an assault decided by the strength roll knocks a fortified territory down a level, and rebuilding it costs a quarter of its price; a fallen territory loses its fortification; a defense won in person keeps it.
7. Station: free; up to 2 reserve pilots (roster pilots not fielded) per threatened territory, DEFENSE from their rating, up to +1 for the pair; they return when that assault is decided; a stationed reserve can't garrison the town.
8. Lunar Pass perks (built behind the Pass flag; live when the store opens in Session 8): a second fortification slot, and fortifications cost 20% less and build 20% faster.
9. Grand Offensive: about three territories (planning pick). Not started by the end of its 48-hour window: it launches and each target is decided by strength with its extra THREAT. It is the one event that may change more than one territory in a tick, inside the limits. Skipped while the front is frozen. The town as a target comes with 7T.
10. Rewards: war battles pay by the approved formula (par minutes x 225 x the ring's danger: Heartland and Rear 1.0, Frontier 1.2, Warfront 1.45); a territory defense pays the existing "Defense victory (sector)" value, 675. Only wins pay (a failed or abandoned battle pays its verified kills only); one claim per assault; battles you didn't fight pay nothing; DAILY_CAP 50,000 and 120 paying outcomes an hour; league and Command Base multipliers as usual; spoils as other Conquest battles of the same danger. Holding territory: supply +2% production (materials and CP only, never points) per held Frontier or Warfront territory, max +16%; its fast-travel node, veins and missions; FRONT readiness. Any bonus is a PROPOSAL.
11. Pace: no war system can be maxed by a regular free player in under about a year; an economy-model test with every bonus on, supply included, fails if faster.
12. Alerts (Session 7 builds them; 7T reuses them): website first, with opt-in browser notifications (computers and Android; on iPhone and iPad only for a site added to the Home Screen, so those players get a one-time "Add LUNAR to your Home Screen to get war alerts" card), asked from a tap after the pilot's first war battle, never at signup. Telegram through the existing bot. At most 3 a day, none in the pilot's local night (the client reports its time zone), several events in one alert, only fights the pilot can still join plus the Grand Offensive announcement, on/off in settings. Nothing for the owner to set up: create the website's push keys yourself.

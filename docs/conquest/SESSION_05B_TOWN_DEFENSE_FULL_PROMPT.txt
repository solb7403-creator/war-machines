SETTLED OWNER DECISIONS (Nov 8 2026)
1. TOWN DEFENSE, not a bunker. The plot is a town that grows with the Command Center: CC1 = today's 45x45, growing in steps
   to about 3x the area at CC10 (about 78x78 cells). Settler homes and town structures fill the new districts as it grows
   (settler architecture kit from Session 5), so CC10 reads as a real settlement, not an empty field.
2. VITAL BUILDINGS are the enemy's objective. The Command Center is the primary objective: destroy it and the raid wins.
   Reactor, Storage Depot and Science Lab are secondary vitals: losing one cuts that building's output until repaired. The
   player places vitals ANYWHERE in the town (spread out for coverage, or clustered behind the best walls).
3. ENEMIES ATTACK THE WHOLE TOWN. Raiders path toward the Command Center through the cheapest route (weighted by wall HP and
   turret threat), attack anything that blocks them or is in range on the way (walls, turrets, production, homes, vitals),
   and break through the weakest wall. Applies to the Defense Drill now (still no rewards) and is the model async raids
   (Session 7) will reuse.
4. WALLS AS LARGE AS YOU WANT. Base wall cap 600 cells at CC1 rising to 2,000 at CC10
   (600, 756, 911, 1067, 1222, 1378, 1533, 1689, 1844, 2000). WALL EXTENSIONS add up to +50% of the current level's base cap;
   they can be EARNED (Foundry research with rarer ores, defended raids / drill milestones) or BOUGHT as a store item
   (capacity only: strength still comes from composite and ore). Checkout stays DISABLED until payments are certified.
5. WALL COMPOSITE: a new base material. Every pilot starts with a stock (target: enough to wall the CC1 perimeter, about 180
   cells, on day one). A new FOUNDRY building turns alloy + plot regolith into more composite (production + cap like the
   Refinery; upgradable). The old 5-tier wall table is replaced.
6. ORE BLENDS: for each wall section the player picks an ore (Ferrite, Ilmenite, Armalcolite, Tranquillityite, Selenite,
   Moonheart) and a blend level (none / light / medium / heavy, ore per cell rising with the level). Three stats scale in
   direct relation to the ore's tier and amount: STRENGTH (max HP), DURABILITY (damage reduction) and REFLECTIVENESS (share
   of incoming energy/kinetic damage bounced back). Composite alone is modest; Ferrite is a cheap step up; Moonheart heavy is
   unmistakably elite (target: about 5x composite HP, high damage reduction, about 35% reflect). Rare ores stay EARN-ONLY
   (only common and uncommon minerals are ever sold, owner decision Nov 7). Walls show their blend visually (tint, sheen,
   veins, glass glint for Selenite, a deep glow for Moonheart).
7. ELEVEN TURRET TYPES, each with its own 3D model, firing effects and a clear role:
   existing Cannon, Laser, Missile (anti-air), Mortar (long splash, min range), plus
   TWIN-BARREL MACHINE GUN (fast, light, great vs swarms), HEAVY SIEGE CANNON (slow, huge single hits, vs armor),
   ROCKET POD (salvos, area), LASER CANNON (charged beam, high burst), TESLA ARC COIL (chains between nearby enemies),
   RAILGUN (pierces everything in a line), FLAME PROJECTOR (short cone, burns).
   Unlock them across CC levels so early choices stay simple; turret cap scales with the town. Upgrades I-V as today.
8. TERRAIN TEMPLATES stay (no world-map placement): Crater Rim, River Delta, Ridge, Canyon, plus 2-3 new ones with real
   defensive character (e.g. Highland Plateau, Ice Basin, Ash Field). At town scale the terrain shapes the whole defense.
9. FAST WALL TOOLS so 600+ cells never means 600 taps: line, rectangle, ring, copy/paste a section, undo; blend applies to a
   whole drawn section. Live count "WALLS 412 / 600 (+EXT)" and composite/ore cost before confirming.
10. DEV MODE covers everything new: unlimited composite, ores and extensions, instant builds, never ranked.
11. STORE (fair revenue; all DISABLED until payments are certified, shown honestly as unavailable): wall extension packs
    (capacity), wall and turret skins, Foundry speed-up, second builder. Never airdrop points, never rare-or-better ores.
